The 4 Phase Gates

A product is not "shippable" until it clears all 4 gates. Skip a gate, pay it back later with returns and complaints.

01
Mechanical
Works smoothly, repeatably, 10 times in a row without intervention.
02
Stranger
Someone who didn't build it can assemble it from your manual alone.
03
Sale
5+ real strangers have paid real money for it on at least one channel.
04
Margin
You've shipped enough to know true unit cost — including returns and damage.

0%
Before designing 4 interoperable products, lock the shared interface with one test coupon. v1.1 is born here. This is the only block that must finish before product CAD begins.
  • Buy digital calipers (0.01mm), steel rule, vernier gaugeA1h

  • CAD the test coupon — every hole, slot, motor pocket, M3 grid, rail joint on ONE A4 sheetA3h
  • Add kerf compensation table to CAD (0.1mm typical for 4mm ply)A1h
  • Identify 3 Rajkot laser vendors — visit, compare quality, get sample cutsA2h
  • Source N20 motors (60/100/300 RPM), 4mm shafts, M3 bolts/nuts/washersA1h
  • Order birch ply (4mm and 6mm), grade B-BB, ≥15 sheets for prototypingA1h
  • Pick up test coupon — inspect cut edge quality (charring, smoothness)A1h
  • Caliper EVERY dimension, log delta from spec in a spreadsheetA2h
  • Fit-test: shaft slip, motor snap-fit, M3 thread engagement, rail mateA1h
  • Write MOTRIX-Link v1.1 spec document: nominal, tolerance, fit class for each interfaceA2h
  • Build "MOTRIX-Link Toolkit" — golden reference parts kept on the benchA1h
